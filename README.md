# CineMatcher 🎬✨

**CineMatcher** is your trusty movie matchmaker. Don’t know what to watch next? Just throw a movie title into the search bar, and let CineMatcher work its magic. Powered by the TMDb API, it digs through the movie world and returns a curated list of films that’ll fit right into your vibe. It's like a movie-buddy that knows your tastes better than you do. 😎

## What it Does
- **Movie Search**: Tell it one movie you like, and it’ll send you a list of related films. Simple.
- **Movie Deep-Dive**: Get the full scoop—posters, genres, ratings, and more—because who doesn’t love the details?
- **Responsive Layout**: Whether you're on your couch with a tablet or sitting at your desk, CineMatcher adjusts to your screen size.

## How it Works (In Case You’re Curious)
1. You toss in a movie title you like.
2. CineMatcher scours the TMDb API for that movie, grabbing the genres and other tidbits.
3. With that info, it pulls up a batch of similar films you might love.
4. Each movie comes with all the juicy deets—posters, release dates, ratings, and overviews—so you’ll know what you’re in for.

## Tech Behind the Magic
- **HTML**: Lays the groundwork, obviously.
- **CSS**: Makes it look good. Flexbox? Hover effects? Yep, it’s all there.
- **JavaScript**: The brains. Fetching data from TMDb, displaying recommendations, and making it all interactive.

## Wanna Try It?

1. Clone the repo to your machine:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/CineMatcher.git
   ```

2. Get your hands on an API key from [TMDb](https://www.themoviedb.org/).

3. Paste your shiny new key into `script.js` where it says `const API_KEY = 'your_api_key';`.

4. Open `index.html` in your browser and start exploring the movie universe. 🎥🌌
